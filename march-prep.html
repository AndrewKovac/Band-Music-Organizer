Set up your instruments and page sizing

Everything below drives the whole run. The instrument list is your vocabulary — it powers both the tagging shortcuts and the OCR guesses, so name the books exactly how you want the output files named, and list the label variants you actually see on the page.

Instrument list

One per line: Canonical name | alias, alias, alias. Lines starting with # are ignored. Aliases are matched case-insensitively against the corner label.

Page & binding

Output is US Letter (8.5×11). Music is trimmed to its ink, then scaled to fill the space minus the binding gutter.

Gutter is the punch margin kept clear on the binding edge. With double-sided printing the gutter alternates each page — holes on the binding edge for fronts, on the opposite edge for backs — so the punch lines up through the whole stack. Trim removes whitespace so the notes get as big as possible.

Add your song PDFs

Drop in one PDF per song (multi-part files are fine — each page being a different player is exactly what the tagger sorts out). On import, pages holding two stacked parts are split automatically, and each part is framed as Lyre (two share a page) or Full page. Use the toggle to set a song's frames in bulk, or change any part individually in Tag.
